# Chapter 9: Technology & Data Variations

## Core Idea

Separate changed behavior from alternative means of performing the same behavior.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Technology variation**: the same interaction can occur by different channel or implementation mechanism.
- **Data variation**: the same information is provided or represented in different acceptable forms.

## How to Apply

Place variations in their own list when the goal, scenario meaning, and guarantees remain the same. Use extensions only when conditions lead to substantively different behavior or outcomes.

## Key Takeaways

1. Do not inflate extensions with channel choices.
2. Keep the behavioral contract stable while documenting permitted variability.
3. Link detailed formats and field rules rather than embedding them in use-case prose.
