# Chapter 15: Business Process Modeling

## Core Idea

Business use cases describe enterprise behavior at a wider scope, making organizational roles and lifecycle goals visible before committing to a particular system boundary.

## How to Apply

Set the enterprise or department as the SuD and keep the primary actor outside it. Use summaries to show end-to-end value; link system use cases beneath them when systems are designed or changed.

## Key Takeaways

1. Enterprise scope is different from system scope.
2. Business processes provide context for system requirements.
3. Do not confuse a white-box internal workflow with an external behavioral contract.
