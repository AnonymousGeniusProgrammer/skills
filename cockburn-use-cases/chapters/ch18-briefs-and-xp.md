# Chapter 18: Use Case Briefs and eXtreme Programming

## Core Idea

Short briefs can provide enough shared behavioral understanding in fast-feedback environments; expand only where a story, risk, or team boundary needs precision.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Use case brief**: a 2–6 sentence reminder of major activity and failures.
  - When to use: estimation, COTS evaluation, early planning, or high-trust teams with continuous stakeholder contact.
  - How: capture primary goal, key flow, and meaningful risk without template ceremony.

## Key Takeaways

1. Briefs are intentional low precision, not incomplete prose.
2. Continuous discussion and examples can carry details that a document does not.
3. Expand selectively when communication bandwidth is insufficient.
